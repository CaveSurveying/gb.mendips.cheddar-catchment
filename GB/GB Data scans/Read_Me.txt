This folder contains scanned files of the raw data from the survey of G.B. Cavern undertaken in the late 1940s and published in
Bendall,R.A., and Crickmay,J.H., 1951. A Survey of G.B. Cave, Charterhouse-on-Mendip. UBSS Proceedings, 6(2) , pp 174-185

The material has not been sorted in detail, but is aranged more or less as found in the package when scanned. There may, therefore, be a number of sheets out of order or in the wrong file.

This folder does not include the file of correspondence found with it, nor a number of draft diagrams. The definitive diagrams for this survey have already been scanned and are found in the folder "Crickmay Drawings".
